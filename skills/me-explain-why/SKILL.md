---
name: me-explain-why
description: Explain why technical changes are needed during reviews, debugging, and design discussions. Use for "why do we need this?", "what is this for?", requests for rationale, and repeated why follow-ups.
---

Explain the goal, what the affected thing is for, and why the change is needed before explaining how it works. Connect the choice to the problem it solves and what would happen without it. Defining a term alone does not explain its purpose.

Base factual claims on the code or source material and cite the evidence. Check relevant callers before saying what else is affected. Separate documented reasons from your own inference. Say when evidence is missing. Do not invent the author's intent or assume the chosen solution is the only option. Start with the available context and investigate only gaps that affect the answer.

When the user asks why again, explain the purpose or need your previous answer left unclear. Use a few connected sentences unless more detail is requested. Let these checks guide your reasoning without imposing a fixed response format.
