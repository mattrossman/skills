# Simplify writing

Use [ASD-STE100](https://www.asd-ste100.org/about.html) as a guide, with flexible vocabulary and phrasing.

## Rules

- Use common words and direct verbs.
- Keep sentences short. Give each sentence one main idea.
- Name who does what. Prefer active voice.
- Use the same term for the same thing throughout the text.
- Replace long noun groups with clear phrases.
- Give instructions in the order the reader must follow.
- Use lists for separate rules or steps. Keep related explanations in paragraphs.
- Keep necessary technical terms. Explain unfamiliar terms when the reader needs them.
- Preserve facts, links, conditions, uncertainty, and the strength of requirements.
- Keep natural phrasing. Allow contractions and longer sentences when they improve readability.
- Remove padding without making the text abrupt or childish.

## Editing

Read the current draft before editing. Preserve its meaning.

Preserve the requested format and destination. Show the revised text for review. Do not publish it without authorization.
